# WorkForce AI Pro

## HR Attrition Prediction & Workforce Intelligence Platform

WorkForce AI Pro is an AI-powered Human Resources management and workforce intelligence platform designed to help organizations monitor employees, analyze workforce trends, predict employee attrition risk, and support data-driven HR decision-making.

The platform combines traditional HR management capabilities with Artificial Intelligence, predictive analytics, workforce monitoring, performance analytics, recommendations, notifications, reporting, and real-time communication.

---

## Overview

WorkForce AI Pro provides a centralized platform for managing and analyzing the employee lifecycle.

The system enables HR administrators and authorized users to:

- Manage employee information
- Monitor attendance
- Manage leave requests
- Process payroll
- Predict employee attrition risk using machine learning
- Monitor workforce risk
- Generate AI-powered HR recommendations
- Analyze workforce analytics
- Track employee performance
- Generate workforce reports
- Manage notifications
- Communicate through real-time chat
- Monitor workforce stability
- Manage employee documents and datasets

The goal of the platform is to transform HR operations from manual administration into an intelligent, data-driven workforce management system.
